Reading the chart

Sector
One of the four reward gauges: Stable, Project, Bluechip, Single
Price gap
Two pools disagreeing on a token's price by more than their fees. It can be a real mispricing or an error, and it is not a promise of profit: fees, slippage and thin pools can swallow it
Framing
Double-click a system (double-tap on a phone) to swing the view down behind it, with the Moon beyond. Double-click the Moon, or press Reset, to step back
Planet
One token. Click a planet to see its price, how much of it is on Terra, and every system it orbits in
System
A liquidity pool, drawn as two bodies in orbit: one for each token in the pair. A single-token stake is a lone star
Mass
The deeper the pool, the larger its bodies
Outer orbit
Pools outside the Alliance. They float beyond the rim with no stem and no trade lane
Settled
The part of a pool staked through the Alliance, which is what earns its rewards
Hollow
Known to the Alliance but not priced or not readable yet
Brass halo
A crown system: it holds liquidity owned by the chain itself
Yield
Estimated LUNA rewards per year on what is staked, less the Alliance's yearly take. Swap fees are not counted
Tribute
Tokens offered to the houses that vote for a system
Houses
Holders who lock LUNA to vote
Cycle
One weekly epoch of votes and rewards
